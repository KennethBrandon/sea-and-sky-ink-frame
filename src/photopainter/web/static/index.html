    Wi-Fi networks

    The networks the frame joins on its own. Add the one at the house it's going to and it connects on arrival; Tailscale follows. The frame has 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only: on a 5 GHz-only network it just never connects, so check the band before you go.

      If the frame loses its Wi-Fi

      It carries on, reconnects by itself and shows a small mark. For a new router or password, hold the back button for a second: the frame shows a QR code that sets it up from a phone. This line goes on that screen.

      The buttons on the back

      Two small round black buttons sit side by side on the back of the frame, just above the small green circuit board. With the frame on the wall, face the picture and reach behind the top edge, about a quarter of the way in from the right: the one nearer the right is Forward.

      What a press does

      On the scheduleIn photos only
      ForwardThe next screenThe next photo
      BackThe screen beforeThe photo before
      Forward held for a second, then let goInto photos onlyBack to the schedule
      Back held for a second, then let goWi-Fi setup, if the frame has lost its Wi-Fi
      Both held for a secondLow power on or off

      Give it time

      The picture takes about 30 seconds to change, and it flickers and flashes while it does. That's normal for this kind of screen. A press during that time is ignored, so wait for the picture to settle before you press again.

      On the schedule

      This is how the frame starts: it changes by itself through the day. Forward and Back jump along the day's screens (or the night's), and it carries on from there until the next sunrise, sunset or midnight. If that lands on a photo, it's the next photo, whichever button you pressed. If only one screen is set for this time of day, there's nowhere to go, so a press does nothing.

      Photos only

      A slideshow of your photos, day and night. It has no end of its own: it stays on until you hold Forward for a second and let go, or tap Auto under Show on this page.

      Low power

      For when the frame runs on its battery, in a power cut or unplugged. With it on, Wi-Fi and this page switch off to make the charge last. While the frame is plugged in, nothing changes until the next time it's unplugged. A small crescent near the bottom right of the picture shows it's set.

      If this page has gone away, hold both buttons for a second, or plug the frame in. Either brings it back within a minute or so.

      When the battery is nearly flat, the frame settles on a photo and the buttons can't change the picture until it's plugged in again.

      No Wi-Fi

      If the Wi-Fi goes down, you don't need to do anything. The tides, the sky and your photos carry on without it, and the frame reconnects by itself when the Wi-Fi comes back. After about 10 minutes, this small mark appears near the bottom right of the picture the next time it changes:

      If the Wi-Fi stays away because of a new router, a new password or a move to a new house, hold Back for a second and let go. The picture changes to a QR code. Point your phone's camera at it and tap Join, then pick your home Wi-Fi and type its password. Your phone will drop off while the frame tries it, so watch the frame: in about a minute it says whether that worked. Any button calls it off, and it turns itself off after 15 minutes if nobody uses it.

      This page lives on the frame, so it won't open while the frame has no Wi-Fi.
